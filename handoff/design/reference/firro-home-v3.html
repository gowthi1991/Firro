How it works

A day in a Firro kitchen.

Customers change their plans. Firro turns those changes into tonight's lock, the morning's prep sheet and the lunch run — on its own.

  1. All day

    Customers manage their plans.

    Skip, swap or pause from the customer app — inside the cutoffs you set.

  2. Cutoff

    Tomorrow locks.

    Firro freezes the meal count per dish, per diet, per slot. No late surprises.

  3. Morning

    The prep sheet is waiting.

    Batch KOTs print for the line, one ticket per parcel for packing.

  4. Meal time

    Out the door, tracked.

    Parcels leave with their own ticket; customers see when lunch is close.

FAQ

Questions kitchens ask us.

Something else? Chat on WhatsApp.

Does the POS work without internet?

Yes. Firro POS keeps taking orders and printing KOTs when the connection drops, and syncs everything once it's back.

What devices do I need?

A tablet or desktop for the counter and a network thermal printer. Kitchen Admin runs in any browser.

Who owns our data?

You do. Your menu, orders and subscriber list stay yours.

Where does the nutrition data come from?

From the raw ingredients you link to each recipe. Firro adds them up by weight, so the numbers follow your actual recipe, not a generic dish.

How much will batch cooking save us?

It depends on your volume, menu and how you cook today. Bring a week of orders to the demo and we'll work it out with your numbers.

What does it cost?

It depends on your kitchen's size and which apps you need. Book a demo and we'll work it out together.
